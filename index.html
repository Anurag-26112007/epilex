EPILEX

A Signal Detected, A Life Protected

A free, open-source, software-only system that detects seizure activity from EEG signals or a simple webcam and alerts caregivers within seconds.

Watch demo video View on GitHub

The problem

Seizures strike without warning, and help often arrives late, especially when the person is asleep or alone.

10-12M

people live with epilepsy in India.

Night

Unwitnessed seizures can lead to serious injury or SUDEP.

Cost

Clinical wearables are expensive and out of reach for many households.

Gap

Research EEG models detect, but never alert anyone.

The gap: no free, live, software-only system connects seizure detection to caregiver alerts.

How it works

Two inputs, one decision engine, many alert channels.

1. InputEEG replay (public CHB-MIT data) or live webcam
→
2. DetectEEG model on 2-second windows, or MediaPipe body tracking
→
3. ConfirmNeeds several consecutive hits, then a cooldown
→
4. AlertSiren, push, email, SMS, plus a caregiver note in Hindi or English

Mode A: EEG

Brain-signal windows are classified as seizure or normal with a confidence score. Evaluated on patients the model has never seen.

Mode B: Webcam

Tracks body landmarks and looks for sustained, rhythmic shaking of several limbs. Video is processed locally and never stored.

Caregiver note

A local open-weight model rephrases a vetted first-aid template. It never invents new medical advice.

Open-source stack

Every layer is free to run, inspect and improve.

EPILEX is an assistive research prototype, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose. The camera mode is demonstrated on acted movement only. Results are reported exactly as measured.
